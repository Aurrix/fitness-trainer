## Progress photos

- Track progress photos from Progression, alongside the Growth Map.
- Take a photo with a pose guide, review it, then save or retake it. You can also upload photos from your library.
- Browse one photo at a time with a touch-friendly timeline slider. Photos use their capture date when available, and can be downloaded or removed.

## Workout logging

- Mark each set to increase, keep, or decrease its weight. The recommendation is saved with workout history and shown for that set in the next workout.
- Add set notes and tags such as drop sets, failure, partial reps, or assisted reps by pressing and holding a set.
- Double-tap a weight or reps input to open its previous set values and apply one to the current set.
- Workout set controls use a more compact layout, and target cards prioritize the highest-volume values.
