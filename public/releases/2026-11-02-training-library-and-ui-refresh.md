## UI and workout logging

- Refactored the workout, library, exercise-detail, and replacement screens with cleaner, mobile-first layouts.
- Workout logging now fits each set on one row, with collapsed exercise cards, an Up next indicator, and a compact overview while reordering.
- Increase, Hold, and Decrease advice is available for each set, including prescribed sets. Tap a set number for effort, notes, tags, history, and removal.

## Expanded training library

- Added more exercises and training programs.
- Added generated exercise videos, with compact animated previews in workout cards and program previews.

## Exercise details and replacement

- Swipe between video and front/back muscle targets in the exercise overview. Open the Performance tab for personal records, trends, and previous sets.
- Inspect a replacement exercise and its past performance before confirming a swap.

## Video playback

- Visible exercise videos autoplay without controls and resume after tab or view changes. Manual pauses and reduced-motion preferences remain respected.
