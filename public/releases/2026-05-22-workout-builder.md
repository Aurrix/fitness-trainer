## Workout logging

- Finished and skipped exercises now expose explicit edit and remove actions at the end of the row.
- Exercise details use a flatter muscle visualizer layout and move difficulty/equipment metadata into the subtitle area.
- Exercise alternatives now focus on same-target primary and secondary muscle matches in compact rows.

## Custom programs

- Program goals, levels, and common tags use selectable options.
- Custom plans can be organized by weeks and days.
- Builder days support moving weeks, days, and exercises.
- Tapping a muscle in the builder visualizer adds the next unused matching exercise for that day.
